This is everything your browser is willing to tell a website. Drivers, serial numbers and temperatures are not available.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Every test runs inside your browser and nothing is saved on a server. The speed test only talks to Cloudflare's speed servers to measure your connection, and your microphone audio is analyzed live on your device and never recorded or uploaded.

Why does the microphone test ask for permission?

Browsers always ask before a website can use your microphone. Click Allow to start the test. You can turn the permission off at any time in your browser's site settings.

Why is my speed test result lower than my internet plan?

Plans are usually advertised as up to a certain speed. Wi-Fi signal, other devices, a VPN, browser extensions and the distance to the test server all lower the result. For the most accurate number, use a network cable, pause other downloads and run the test two or three times.

What do latency and jitter mean?

Latency is how long data takes to travel to a server and back, in milliseconds. Jitter is how much that delay changes from moment to moment. Low latency and low jitter feel best for video calls and online games.

How much data does the speed test use?

The test downloads for about 8 seconds and uploads for about 6 seconds, so faster connections use more data, often 100 MB or more. Avoid running it on a metered mobile plan.

Why are some keys not detected in the keyboard test?

Your browser or operating system can reserve some keys, such as the Windows key, media keys or PrintScreen. Many keyboards also limit how many keys can be pressed at once, and the Max NKRO number shows the most keys held together in this test.

How do I check my monitor for dead pixels?

Open the Monitor test and start the fullscreen color test. Click through black, white, red, green, blue and gray, and look for dots that stay the wrong color or never light up. Clean the screen first so dust isn't mistaken for a pixel.

Does DiagOne work on phones and tablets?

The speed, monitor, audio and microphone tests work on most phones and tablets. The keyboard and typing tests need a physical keyboard, and the mouse test needs a mouse or trackpad.

Why can't DiagOne show my CPU, drivers or battery health?

For your privacy and security, browsers do not let websites read your hardware details, drivers or system files. Those scans need a desktop program, so this website sticks to tests a browser can run safely.
